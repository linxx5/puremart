# Puremart Marketplace

> Buy with Confidence. Sell with Trust.

A trusted wholesale & retail marketplace with escrow payments, delivery tracking, and AI product safety.

## Overview

Puremart is a digital marketplace where individuals and businesses can buy and sell products in both wholesale and retail quantities.

What makes Puremart different is **trust**:

- AI helps identify fake, unsafe, suspicious, or harmful products
- Admin makes the final decision on all safety matters (AI flags, admin decides)
- Payments are protected through escrow
- Buyers can track deliveries end-to-end
- Buyers and sellers are protected through a dispute system

The goal is a marketplace where people feel safe buying products online — combining marketplace + wholesale + African commerce + escrow protection + AI safety.

## User Types

Puremart has just 3 user types:

### 1. Buyer
Purchases for personal, family, business, or bulk use. Can buy in retail and wholesale quantities. Flow: search → compare → cart → quantity → wholesale/retail → delivery → pay → track.

### 2. Seller
One seller account with 2 modes:
- **Retail Seller:** small-scale sellers, personal products, shops, SMEs, retail businesses, registered brands. Sells retail quantities.
- **Wholesale Seller:** distributors, manufacturers, bulk suppliers. Sells bulk with MOQs, bulk discounts, carton prices, negotiations, and custom quotations.

A seller can be Retail only, Wholesale only, or both. Any seller can apply for Verified Seller status.

### 3. Admin
Controls seller approval, product approval, AI review decisions, disputes, categories, marketplace rules, and user management.

## Key Features

- **Seller verification:** identity, business info, contact, address, registration. Admin approval. Verified Seller badge.
- **Product categories:** Food & Beverages, Health, Fashion, Electronics, Home Appliances, Beauty, Agriculture, Tools, Books, Cleaning, Automotive, Others. Admin can turn categories ON/OFF.
- **AI product safety:** checks images, descriptions, brand, ingredients, expiry, certifications, price anomalies, seller history, complaints, duplicates/counterfeits. Status: Passed / Under Review / Rejected. Low-risk = fast approval, Medium = AI + admin, High = mandatory admin.
- **Escrow payments:** buyer pays into Puremart escrow. Released on delivery confirmation, protection-period expiry, or dispute resolution. Flexible: fast release for retail, longer protection for high-value, inspection period for wholesale.
- **Delivery:** seller delivery + Puremart-approved logistics. Tracking: placed → confirmed → processing → packed → shipped → in transit → out for delivery → delivered.
- **Notifications:** order, payment, delivery, approvals, disputes, promos via app, SMS, email, WhatsApp.
- **Disputes:** all orders. Buyer reports fake/damaged/wrong/missing/expired/undelivered with photos/videos. Seller responds, admin decides: full/partial refund, replacement, return+refund, release to seller, or settlement.
- **Reviews & PureTrust Score:** buyers rate product, seller, delivery. Score from deliveries, complaints, quality, ratings, verification level (e.g. 95/100 Trusted Seller).
- **AI extras:** smart search (e.g. "Best toothpaste under ₦5,000"), recommendations, fraud detection.
- **Admin dashboard:** users, products, disputes, approvals, reports, categories, AI alerts, escrow.
- **Revenue:** transaction fee, seller subscriptions, featured listings, logistics commission, escrow fees.
