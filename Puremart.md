# **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

# **Puremart Marketplace**

### **A Trusted Wholesale & Retail Marketplace with Escrow, Delivery Tracking and AI Product Safety**

**Version:** 1.0  
 **Product Name:** Puremart Marketplace  
 **Tagline:** *Buy with Confidence. Sell with Trust.*

---

## **1\. Product Vision**

Puremart is a digital marketplace where individuals and businesses can buy and sell products in both wholesale and retail quantities.

Puremart's key difference is trust:

* AI helps identify fake, unsafe, suspicious, or harmful products.  
* Admin makes the final decision on all safety matters.  
* Payments are protected through escrow.  
* Buyers can track deliveries.  
* Buyers and sellers are protected through a dispute system.

The goal is to create a marketplace where people feel safe buying products online.

---

# **2\. Core Objectives**

Puremart aims to:

* Enable retail and wholesale buying and selling.  
* Protect buyers from fake or harmful products.  
* Protect sellers from fraudulent buyers.  
* Secure payments through escrow.  
* Provide transparent delivery tracking.  
* Support dispute resolution.  
* Build trust through seller verification and product reviews.

---

# **3\. User Types**

Puremart has just 3 user types: Buyer, Seller, and Admin.

## **1. Buyer**

People purchasing products for:

* Personal use  
* Family use  
* Business use  
* Bulk purchases

Buyers can buy in both retail and wholesale quantities.

---

## **2. Seller**

All sellers fall under one Seller account with 2 seller modes:

### **Retail Seller**

* Small-scale sellers  
* Personal products  
* Shops  
* SMEs  
* Retail businesses  
* Registered businesses and trusted brands

Sells in retail quantities to buyers for personal, family, or business use.

### **Wholesale Seller**

* Distributors  
* Manufacturers  
* Bulk suppliers

Sells in bulk quantities with minimum order quantities, bulk discounts, and custom quotations.

A seller can operate as Retail only, Wholesale only, or both.

Any seller can apply to become a 🟢 Verified Seller (see Section 4).
